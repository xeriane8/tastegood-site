What’s good here?

Good tells you what to order. Instead of rating a restaurant or bar, you rate the thing you ordered — the burger, the old fashioned, the pie — and those ratings add up into what’s worth ordering at each place and what’s good near you.

Join the beta on TestFlight

How it works

1

Rate what you ordered

A photo, a star rating, the place and the item. Food or drink, app or dessert. Takes thirty seconds.

2

See what’s good at a place

Every place gets a page: its top items first, then everything by category. Order the thing people actually come back for.

3

Find what’s good near you

A map of the best items around you, with filters for category, food type, cuisine, price and meal time.

Good is in beta. Reviews from testers are real and count.
